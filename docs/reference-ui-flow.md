# Alpha Plus reference-style driver flow

Alpha Plus uses a map-first driver workspace inspired by the supplied driver
reference, while preserving Alpha branding, data rules and vehicle classes.

## Journey

1. Phone number, OTP and biometric choice
2. Driver identity, licence, documents, vehicle and device setup
3. Review status and approval feedback
4. Offline/online availability workspace
5. Incoming offer with pickup distance, fare, route and payment
6. Accepted ride, passenger contact, arrival and trip start
7. Waiting meter, navigation and trip completion
8. Wallet credit, earnings and trip details
9. Inbox, profile, documents, appearance, safety and settings

## Interaction rules

- Keep operational actions reachable with one hand and at least 48 logical
  pixels high.
- Make online, waiting and active-trip states explicit in text and iconography.
- Keep the next required driver action pinned near the bottom edge.
- Display pickup, destination, fare and payment before accepting an offer.
- Use the same information hierarchy in light and dark themes.
- Never copy third-party vehicle artwork; Alpha assets and icons remain.
